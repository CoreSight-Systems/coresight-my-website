Never miss a call, a lead or a detail.

03 · Quotes

From lead to signed quote in minutes.

Start the quote from the lead — contact and property come across. Apply a pricing package from your own price list, enter measurements, and Good, Better and Best price themselves.

  • Multi-page proposals from your own layouts
  • Quantities calculated from measurements
  • Email and portal link — customers pick and sign online

04 · Field Ops

Done in the field. Seen in the office.

Create your own visit types and decide what each one requires before it can close. Crews see their day on their phone, text the customer if they're running late, and capture readings, photos and signatures.

  • Your visit types, your closeout requirements
  • One-tap ETA and delay texts to the customer
  • The office roster updates live

05 · Invoicing

The invoice writes itself.

When a visit closes with every requirement met, the final invoice is already drafted from the job — line items, tax and the deposit applied. Review it, send it by email and text, and the customer pays by bank or card straight from the link.

Every invoice and payment posts to your books automatically, so you always know what's owed and what's been collected.

  • Deposits drafted the moment a quote is signed
  • Final invoices drafted at visit closeout
  • Pay by link — ACH or card
  • Ledger, trial balance and QuickBooks sync
