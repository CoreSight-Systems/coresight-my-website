Core Call · Smart Lead Intake

Never miss a call. Never miss a lead.Never miss a detail.

Core Call captures every inbound call as a lead — whether it goes to voicemail or a team member answers live. From there, the platform transcribes and summarizes the conversation and automatically updates the customer and job records, so the details capture themselves and nothing slips through.

01 · AI Intake
Core Call pulls every important detail from an incoming call — voicemail or answered live.
02 · Lead Created
A lead is created automatically, with the customer's information already filled in.
03 · Summarized
Every conversation is summarized with the relevant details captured.
04 · One Page
Whoever talks to the customer, the whole team stays on the same page — no missing details, no lost context.

Our business model

CoreSight Systems LLC is a B2B software company. We license our platform to service and trade businesses under a Master Service Agreement — it's a subscription service for companies, not a consumer product.
