Your own system

Your business gets its own private system. Not a spot in a shared one.

Most software puts thousands of companies in one big shared database. CoreSight doesn't. Every client gets a dedicated environment — think of it as your own building instead of a unit in someone else's.

  • A breach elsewhere can't reach you.Every company is walled off on its own system, so a security problem at one business stays at that business — never all of them at once.
  • Another company's busy day never slows you down.Your system's speed is yours — it isn't shared with anyone.
  • It's your data, and you can take it with you.No contracts and no lock-in. If you ever leave, your history goes with you.
  • Backed up every day, watched around the clock.A mistake or an outage never means starting over, and problems get caught before your customers notice.
